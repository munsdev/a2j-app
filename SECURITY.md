# Security

From A to Job runs entirely in the browser and keeps everything people enter in their own browser storage.
A security problem here usually means a way for someone else to read or change that data: script injection
through a pasted AI answer, an imported backup file or a link, or a weakness in how the site is served.

## Reporting a problem

Please do not open a public issue. Email **casey@muns.dev** with:

- what the problem is and what an attacker could do with it,
- the steps to reproduce it,
- the browser and version.

You will get an answer within a week. Once it is fixed, you are welcome to be credited in the release notes.

## Supported version

Only the version live at https://a2j.app is supported. Self-hosted copies should follow the `main` branch.
